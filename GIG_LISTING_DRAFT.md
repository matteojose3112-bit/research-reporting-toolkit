## Full description
I help entrepreneurs and small teams turn research questions into organized, decision-ready documents.

**What I can deliver**
- Focused web and desk research
- Competitor and service comparisons
- Company, market, or topic research from public sources
- Summaries of reports and readable documents
- Executive summaries, key findings, and action lists
- Source logs with links and access dates
- Excel-compatible comparison tables

**My process**
1. Confirm your question, audience, scope, and deadline.
2. Gather relevant sources, prioritizing official and authoritative material.
3. Record source links and connect claims to evidence.
4. Separate sourced facts from analysis and recommendations.
5. Review the final document for clarity, citations, and limitations.

**What you can expect**
Clear writing, traceable sources, honest treatment of unknowns, and a deliverable matched to the agreed scope. I do not fabricate sources, quotes, scores, or findings.

This service is for general business research and document support. It is not legal, medical, investment, or other regulated professional advice. Extensive proprietary databases, paid-source access, and specialized expert opinions are not included unless agreed beforehand.

## FAQ

**Do you include sources?**  
Yes. Material factual claims are supported with links where available, and a source log can be included.

**Can you compare competitors?**  
Yes. Please provide the options and priorities, or ask me to propose criteria before research begins.

**Do you guarantee that every online claim is true?**  
No source can guarantee that. I prioritize credible sources, document limitations, and flag uncertainty rather than guessing.

**Can you summarize a report you provide?**  
Yes, if the document is readable and you have the right to share it. Please confirm length and format before ordering.

**Do you provide legal, medical, or investment advice?**  
No. I provide general research and document support, not regulated professional advice.

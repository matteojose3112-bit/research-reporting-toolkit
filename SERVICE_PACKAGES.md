# Research & Reporting Services — Starter Packages

These are proposed launch packages for Fiverr/Upwork. Confirm final platform pricing, delivery dates, and scope with each buyer before accepting work.

## 1. Research Brief
**Starter scope:** One focused business question, up to 5 credible sources.
- Concise brief with findings and source links
- Key limitations and open questions
- Short executive takeaway
- Delivery target: 2 business days after scope and sources are confirmed

## 2. Competitor Comparison
**Starter scope:** Up to 3 options and 6 agreed criteria.
- Side-by-side comparison spreadsheet
- Source log with access dates
- Summary of trade-offs and unknowns
- Transparent scoring only when criteria and evidence support it
- Delivery target: 3 business days

## 3. Document Summary
**Starter scope:** One client-provided document up to 30 pages, subject to readable text and rights to share.
- Structured summary
- Key points, decisions, risks, and action items (where present)
- Page or section references when available
- Delivery target: 2 business days

## Intake requirements
Confirm the business question, audience, geography, date range, preferred source types, number of options, evaluation criteria, output format, deadline, and any confidentiality or handling requirements.

## Quality commitments and boundaries
- Cite material factual claims and keep source logs.
- Prefer primary sources and disclose when evidence is incomplete.
- Distinguish source facts from interpretation and recommendations.
- Do not fabricate sources, quotations, findings, or scores.
- This is research and document support, not legal, medical, investment, or other regulated professional advice.
- Keep client documents confidential and do not reuse them in a public portfolio without permission.

# Research & Reporting Quality Review

Use before sending any research brief, comparison, or document summary.

## Brief and scope
- [ ] The research question is specific and the intended audience is clear.
- [ ] Scope, geography, timeframe, and exclusions are stated.
- [ ] The deliverable answers the agreed question.

## Sources and evidence
- [ ] Each material factual claim has a traceable source.
- [ ] Source titles, links, publishers, and access dates are recorded.
- [ ] Primary or authoritative sources were preferred where practical.
- [ ] Sources actually support the claims attributed to them.
- [ ] Conflicting evidence is noted rather than hidden.
- [ ] Estimates, assumptions, opinions, and verified facts are distinguished.
- [ ] Links work and dates are accurate.

## Analysis
- [ ] Comparison criteria are consistent across options.
- [ ] Unknown or undisclosed information is labeled, not guessed.
- [ ] Calculations and units have been checked.
- [ ] Recommendations follow from the evidence and stated priorities.
- [ ] Limitations and unanswered questions are visible.

## Presentation and delivery
- [ ] Executive takeaway is concise and useful.
- [ ] Headings, tables, spelling, and formatting are consistent.
- [ ] File names are clear and version/date are correct.
- [ ] No confidential, personal, or unauthorized information is included.
- [ ] Demo samples are clearly labeled as demonstrations.
- [ ] Requested format and delivery instructions have been followed.

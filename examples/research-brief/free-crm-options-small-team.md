# Research Brief: Free CRM Options for a Very Small Team

> **Portfolio demonstration.** Prepared from public vendor documentation checked on 2026-10-29. This is a limited feature overview, not an exhaustive product test or personalized purchasing recommendation. Plan features and limits can change; verify current terms before making a decision.

**Audience:** Owner or operations lead at a small service business  
**Question:** What should a very small team consider when comparing the free CRM editions from HubSpot and Zoho CRM?  
**Scope:** Official vendor pages, free editions only, USD context where pricing is stated. Paid plans, independent reviews, data-processing terms, and hands-on testing are outside this brief.

## Executive takeaway

Both vendors advertise a free CRM option, but their stated user limits and feature descriptions differ. HubSpot's free CRM page describes a two-user limit and 1,000 contacts, with contact and deal management, tasks, pipeline management, and reporting dashboards. Zoho's pricing page describes a free edition for up to three users, with contact management, follow-up reminders, workflow automation, sales activities, and standard reports. These published descriptions can narrow the shortlist, but a buyer should test the exact workflow and confirm limits before importing customer data.

## Findings

### 1. HubSpot emphasizes a unified customer record and pipeline workflow

HubSpot's official free CRM page describes contact management, deal tracking, tasks and activities, pipeline management, reporting dashboards, CRM import, ticketing, and app integrations. The same page states that its free CRM supports up to two users and 1,000 contacts. [Source S01](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm)

**Implication:** It is worth testing when the team's priority is a clear customer history and visibility into leads or deals. Confirm whether the user and record limits suit the intended team.

### 2. Zoho lists a free edition for up to three users

Zoho's official pricing page lists the Free edition at US$0 and describes it as free for up to three users. Listed capabilities include contact management, follow-up reminders, workflow automation, tasks, meetings and calls, standard reports, data import, and limited data export. [Source S02](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html)

**Implication:** It is worth testing when an additional user seat or built-in follow-up workflow matters. Verify the current limits on exports, automation actions, integrations, and records before relying on them.

### 3. The public pages are not enough to declare a universal winner

The vendor pages describe different feature sets and limits, and the pages are marketing and pricing sources rather than independent tests. This review did not test usability, data export quality, integration behavior, support responsiveness, or how either product performs with a real service-business workflow.

**Implication:** Select a finalist based on requirements and a short pilot, not on this feature list alone.

## At-a-glance comparison

| Evaluation area | HubSpot Free CRM | Zoho CRM Free |
|---|---|---|
| Published free user limit | Up to 2 users (S01) | Up to 3 users (S02) |
| Published contact limit | 1,000 contacts (S01) | Confirm current record limits in Zoho's live plan documentation |

| Import/export notes | CRM import is listed; check current export requirements (S01) | Import is listed; free export is limited according to the pricing page (S02) |

## Suggested decision method

1. Write down the required number of users and approximate number of contacts.
2. List the team's must-have steps: capture a lead, assign an owner, record a call, schedule a follow-up, move a deal stage, and report on open leads.
3. Test the same sample workflow in both products using dummy data.
4. Check export options, integrations, permission controls, and current plan limits.
5. Record any feature that requires a paid plan as a cost or limitation.
6. Choose the product that meets the must-haves with the least friction for the team.

## Recommendation

Start by testing both free editions against the same short workflow if the team is small enough to fit their published free limits. If a third user is essential, Zoho's listed three-user limit may be a practical differentiator; if the team prioritizes HubSpot's described pipeline and reporting workflow, test that experience directly. Neither point establishes an overall winner without understanding the team's needs and verifying the current plans.

## Limitations

- Information was taken from official vendor pages only; no independent sources or hands-on product tests were used.
- Product pages, feature limits, and terms can change.
- The free plan descriptions may not cover all account-specific restrictions, integrations, or regional terms.
- This brief is general information, not legal, security, or procurement advice.
